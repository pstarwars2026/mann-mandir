Privacy, plainly.

Your mandir belongs to you. Your devotional activity stays on your device.

The app

Mann Mandir is provided by Parashar Shah. It does not require an account and does not collect your devotional activity, offerings or preferences. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics service or app-operated backend.

What stays on your device

Your shrine arrangement and sound and motion preferences are stored locally. Closing the mandir clears current offerings while retaining preferences. You can also clear the arrangement with Reset altar. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to backup or device-management features you control through Apple.

Folding and window measurements are used locally to arrange the shrine and determine when puja is available. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or notification access for its devotional experience.

Artwork and sound

Artwork and sound-generation code are included in the app. No external AI service is called during use. Sound is optional and user controlled.

Apple services

Apple may separately operate App Store, operating-system, backup or diagnostic services under its privacy policy and your device settings. This policy describes Mann Mandir's own behavior.

This website and support

This website adds no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or external font services. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process connection and usage information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact support through GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post is public and is processed by GitHub under its policy. We use your report to respond and improve the app. Please do not include personal information or sensitive devotional details.

Questions and updates

For questions about this policy, open a support issue. This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
