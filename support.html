A little guidance.

Mann Mandir is a personal space for Krishna darshan, aarti, flowers and fruit offerings. Follow the moments that feel right to you.

Available on the App Store

Mann Mandir is a free app for supported iPhones and iPads, with folding features on supported Duo devices. There are no in-app purchases. Download Mann Mandir on the App Store.

Open your mandir

On a supported iPhone or iPad, choose Open mandir. On Duo, open the device fully to make puja available. A partially open Duo posture is for darshan; the thali and offerings return when you open fully.

Move the aarti thali

Make a short stroke on the thali or divo to guide aarti, then lift your finger to rest. You do not need to trace a perfect circle.

For assisted circular aarti, tap the thali. Tap again to stop. You can also find Start circular aarti in the menu under Help & access.

Offer flowers and fruit

Open Offerings and choose a flower or fruit for the altar. Offerings remain around the shrine; they do not cover the Krishna murti. Closing and reopening the mandir starts with a fresh altar.

Sound and stillness

Use the sound control to turn audio on or off. Check your device volume if you cannot hear the bell. Sound begins after you interact with the app.

The app supports reduced motion. With reduced motion enabled, tapping the thali centres it instead of starting continuous circular movement. You can also use Pause to rest the motion.

Keyboard controls

Use the arrow keys to move the thali; hold Shift for smaller movements. Home centres the thali, and Escape pauses. The menu includes Help & access with these controls.

Reset the altar

Use Reset altar in Help & access to clear the arrangement. Closing the mandir also clears the current offerings. Your sound and motion preferences remain on your device.

Get in touch

Report a problem or suggest an improvement through GitHub Issues. Include your device model, OS version, app version and a short description of what happened. For a folding issue, mention portrait or landscape and whether the device was partially or fully open.

Contact support ↗

GitHub reports are public and require a GitHub account. Please leave out personal information, private images and sensitive devotional details.
