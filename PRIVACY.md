# Mann Mandir privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. Provided by Parashar Shah.

Mann Mandir does not require an account and does not collect devotional activity, offerings or preferences. It has no advertising, tracking, analytics service or app-operated backend. Shrine arrangement, sound and motion preferences stay on your device. Closing the mandir or using Reset altar clears current offerings; preferences remain. Removing the app removes local app data, subject to Apple backup or device-management features you control.

Folding and window measurements are used locally. The devotional experience does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location or notification access. Artwork and sound-generation code are bundled; there is no external AI call during use. Sound is optional.

Apple services, including the App Store, operating system, backups and diagnostics, follow Apple's policies and your device settings.

This website adds no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or external font services. GitHub Pages may process connection and usage information under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Support issues are public; we use your report to respond and improve the app. Do not include personal information or sensitive devotional details.

[Read the full policy](https://pstarwars2026.github.io/mann-mandir/privacy.html). [Ask a privacy question](https://github.com/pstarwars2026/mann-mandir/issues/new/choose).

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
